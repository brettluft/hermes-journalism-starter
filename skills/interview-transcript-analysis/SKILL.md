# Interview transcript analysis

Use this workflow for interview transcripts, hearing transcripts, press conferences, and recorded conversations.

## Method

1. Identify the transcript's provenance, date, participants, speaker-label quality, and whether timestamps are available.
2. Note obvious transcription uncertainty before analyzing content.
3. Build a topic outline with timestamp or line references.
4. Extract candidate quotations exactly as written. Include the speaker and timestamp or line range.
5. Separate direct quotations from summaries and interpretations.
6. Flag claims that need external verification.
7. Identify internal contradictions, changes in wording, unanswered questions, and places where context may change the meaning.
8. Produce follow-up questions tied to specific passages.

## Rules

- Never silently repair a quotation. Put uncertain words in brackets or mark them for audio review.
- Do not combine fragments from different parts of the transcript into one quotation.
- Do not infer a speaker's motive, emotion, or intent from wording alone.
- Preserve qualifiers such as "may," "approximately," and "I don't know."
- If the transcript lacks timestamps, use stable line or paragraph references.
- Recommend checking any publication-bound quotation against the recording.
- Minimize unrelated personal information.
